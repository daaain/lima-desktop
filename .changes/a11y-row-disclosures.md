- **A container's, image's or volume's line opens from a button a screen reader can name.** The name and what's under it are one button, wide and tall to click, that says whether its detail is open; the line's Stop, Restart and Remove are buttons of their own beside it. Before, the whole line was one button with the others inside it, read out as several hundred characters of everything on the line. A click anywhere on the line still opens it.
