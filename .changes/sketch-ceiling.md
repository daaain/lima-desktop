- **Every chart above a bar reads alike.** Each now shows a dashed line at its full scale, as a container's memory chart already did, so you can see how close a VM came to its limit at a glance. Pointing at any of them reads out that moment, its average and its peak ("13:32 · 2.4 GiB of 7.8 GiB, at most 2.9 GiB"), where the VM charts and CPU charts used to switch to how long was spent at each level. A compose project's charts are now as tall as its containers'.
