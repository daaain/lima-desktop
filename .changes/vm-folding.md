- **On Containers, each VM's heading shows what it uses, stays in view, and folds.** The heading now draws the VM's memory and CPU, now and over the range, in the same columns as its containers. On a wide window it stays at the top while you scroll through a long list, so you always know whose containers you're looking at. Click it, or its chevron, to fold the VM down to that one row; the name still opens the VM's page. Folds are remembered. A search, or the list narrowed to that VM, still shows its containers.
